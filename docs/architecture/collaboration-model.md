Purpose: Defines what should be documented about sharing and collaboration once the model is designed.

This page will document Scient's collaboration model when it exists. Current
source research and prototype sequencing remain in the
[open-source adaptation map](../research/source-evaluations/open-source-adaptation-map.md#collaboration-sync-sharing-and-cloud);
they are not accepted architecture.

Document here:

- individual identity, affiliation, and membership in multiple organizations,
  groups, and projects
- ad hoc teams that do not depend on institutional membership
- nested institutions, faculties or departments, labs, research groups, and
  subgroups
- cross-institutional and external collaborators
- the distinction between organization roles, group roles, project roles, and
  scientific responsibility
- project membership
- invitations
- roles and permissions
- shared project state
- collaborator presence and simultaneous-editing boundaries
- comments, suggestions, assignments, approvals, and asynchronous review
- version history, comparison, conflicts, attribution, and recovery
- ownership transfer, affiliation changes, member departure, revocation, and
  institutional handoff
- human and agent collaboration on the same project objects
- audit attribution
- cloud-backed sharing behavior
- what remains local-only

Do not use as:

- accepted permission model
- implemented collaboration design
- access-control reference
